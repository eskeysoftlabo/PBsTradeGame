## hub_walkway (v0.19.3, built-in image_gen)
Reference: hub_ground.png (style and footprint).
Use case: stylized-concept. Create a NEW original pedestrian pavement tile for the same isometric dark fantasy trading town as reference. Reference is style and exact footprint guide. Keep identical diamond silhouette, position within square canvas, perspective, thin stone foundation height and transparent alpha surrounding. Replace inner irregular paving with elegant small warm gray limestone cobbles laid in subtle fan-shaped arcs, framed by flat narrow pale limestone slabs. Walkable level surface all the way to all four edges, NO raised curbs or corner pillars, no grass, buildings, objects or text. Muted weathered antique stone, light upper left. No circular medallion. Must connect naturally when many copies are placed side by side with existing paving. True transparent background. Square sprite.
